# Tobu Tobu Girl

`tobu.gb`: Tobu Tobu Girl (2017), a game for the Game Boy by Tangram Games, an arcade platformer: a
girl whose cat has floated off under its balloon chases it up into the sky, bouncing off the heads of
bats, birds and stranger things on the way. Exactly as they released it, on 3 December 2017, at
https://tangramgames.itch.io/tobutobugirl (free, or as much as one chooses to pay):
`tobutobugirl.zip` (SHA-256 `c5ec138071079b9cad227d6fb875ad4e58a77f173ed3cd8758c04c0ab166d6e7`),
which holds it with a README, its manual, its box's art and a sticker; the ROM's SHA-1
`8a8f3c1f21f903ea5a7df8fc8b0a6aa5a602e150`, 256 KB. Its home is http://tangramgames.dk/tobutobugirl/,
its source https://github.com/SimonLarsen/tobutobugirl (its code last changed before the release on
5 November 2017, in commit `22ae4b7`), and its soundtrack
https://potatotan.bandcamp.com/album/tobu-tobu-girl-ost.

As each try starts, and again as the girl reaches a stage's portal, the game turns its whole screen
dark and light in turn, four times in about half a second, and a very small number of people have
seizures after looking at flashing light. The room says that its screen flashes wherever it names
the game: beside its button in the card, on the panel beside the Game Boy, and to a screen reader in
the view's description and its picture's.
