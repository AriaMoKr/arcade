# The ZX Spectrum 48K's ROM

`spectrum.rom`: the Sinclair ZX Spectrum 48K's 16 KB ROM (its BASIC and system), as MAME's
`spectrum` driver loads it (SHA-1 `5ea7c2b824672e914525d1d5c419d71b84a426a2`), copied from Fuse 1.6.0
(`roms/48.rom` in https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/fuse-emulator/fuse/1.6.0/fuse-1.6.0.tar.gz).

Its copyright is Amstrad's, who have given permission for it to be distributed with emulators,
keeping their copyright: its copyright messages are to stay as they are, and nobody may charge for
it. That is Amstrad's statement in comp.sys.amstrad.8bit, as Fuse quotes it in
`roms/README.copyright` and Debian in fbzx's `AMSTRAD` (https://sources.debian.org/src/fbzx/4.8.0-3/AMSTRAD).
The arcade charges nothing for anything.
