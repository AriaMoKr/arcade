# The Vectrex's ROM

`exec_rom.bin`: the Vectrex's 8 KB system ROM, its executive and Mine Storm, the game built into it,
as MAME's `vectrex` driver loads it (SHA-1 `65d07426b520ddd3115d40f255511e0fd2e20ae7`), copied from
vecx, John Hawthorn's Vectrex emulator (`rom.dat` at
https://github.com/jhawthorn/vecx/blob/bc14da8926058bb1e4e487106fd9edc7f68bb697/rom.dat).

Smith Engineering designed the Vectrex, which General Consumer Electronics, then Milton Bradley,
made. Smith Engineering (Jay Smith) "has given permission to make copies of all Vectrex related
materials", its games, manuals and overlays among them, so long as nobody profits: the Vectrex FAQ,
version 6.0 (2006), section 8.1, "Legal issues"
(https://vincenzoscarpa.it/biblioteca/retrofaq/vectrex.htm). The Vectrex's emulators have passed its
ROM on under that permission since. The arcade charges nothing for anything.

In June 2026 the team making a Vectrex Mini said it meant to secure the rights to the launch-era
games Smith Engineering developed, with Jay Smith as its consultant (Time Extension,
https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/06/it-was-magical-the-vectrex-mini-gets-the-blessing-of-the-original-creator-as-game-lineup-is-revealed).
Nothing said there changes the permission above.
